# Pitch Deck Content (12 slides)

Fill in every [BRACKET], then copy each slide's text into the official PLP pitch deck template.
Rule of thumb: one idea per slide, a short headline, 3 bullets at most, one visual.

---

## Slide 1 - Title
**Headline:** [Company name]
**Tagline:** [One line: what you do and for whom]
**Also show:** your name, date, contact email
**Visual:** logo

## Slide 2 - Problem
**Headline:** [The problem in one sentence, from the customer's point of view]
- Who has it: [target customer]
- How bad it is: [one number or real example - cost, time wasted, frequency]
- How they cope today: [current workaround and why it fails]
**Visual:** a photo, quote or statistic that makes the pain obvious

## Slide 3 - Solution
**Headline:** [Company name] helps [customer] do [outcome] by [how]
- [Key benefit 1]
- [Key benefit 2]
- [Key benefit 3]
**Visual:** product mockup or simple before/after

## Slide 4 - Product / How it works
**Headline:** How it works in 3 steps
1. [Customer does X]
2. [Your product does Y]
3. [Customer gets Z]
**Visual:** screenshots, a diagram or a short flow

## Slide 5 - Market Opportunity
**Headline:** A [size] opportunity in [region/segment]
- Total market (TAM): [number + source]
- Reachable market (SAM): [number + how you got it]
- First target (SOM): [number you can realistically win in year 1-2]
**Visual:** three nested circles

## Slide 6 - Business Model
**Headline:** We make money by [revenue source]
- Who pays: [customer / third party]
- Price: [price point or range]
- Per-customer economics: [revenue per customer vs. cost to serve]
**Visual:** simple price table or flow of money

## Slide 7 - Traction / Validation
**Headline:** Early proof that people want this
- [Customers interviewed, survey results, pilot users, waitlist, sales, letters of intent]
- [One strong quote from a real potential customer]
(If you have nothing yet, show your validation plan and what you've learned from customer conversations.)
**Visual:** a small chart or screenshot of results

## Slide 8 - Competition
**Headline:** How we're different
- Direct competitors: [names]
- Indirect alternatives: [what people do instead]
- Our edge: [the one thing hard to copy]
**Visual:** 2x2 map or comparison table with you in the strongest corner

## Slide 9 - Go-to-Market
**Headline:** How we reach our first customers
- Channel 1: [e.g. partnerships, social media, schools, WhatsApp groups]
- Channel 2: [...]
- Cost to acquire a customer (estimate): [number]
**Visual:** funnel or timeline

## Slide 10 - Team
**Headline:** The people behind [Company name]
- [Name] - [role] - [why they're the right person]
- [Name] - [role] - [...]
- Gaps we plan to fill: [advisors or future hires]
**Visual:** photos with one-line bios

## Slide 11 - Financials / The Ask
**Headline:** What we need to reach [milestone]
- Projected revenue, year 1: [number] and key assumptions
- Main costs: [top 2-3]
- Ask: [amount or support needed] to achieve [specific milestone]
**Visual:** use-of-funds pie chart or simple projection

## Slide 12 - Vision / Closing
**Headline:** [Your big-picture vision in one sentence]
- Where we'll be in 3-5 years: [one line]
- Contact: [email / phone / links]
**Visual:** strong closing image + thank you
